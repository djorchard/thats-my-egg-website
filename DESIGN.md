# Website design

## Redesign, 7 November 2026

Daniel rejected the first version as obviously AI and requested research into
Minecraft, Roblox, AAA, indie, and Factorio websites before redesigning it.

Official references reviewed:

- https://www.minecraft.net/en-us: prominent game artwork, compact dark navigation,
  concrete actions, and a separate news area.
- https://www.roblox.com/: familiar compact navigation, clear image grids, and
  direct routes into games. The signed-in homepage was inspected, not copied.
- https://factorio.com/: direct game explanation, practical status information,
  and dated development posts. Blog archive and individual post structure reviewed.
- https://www.stardewvalley.net/: the game's own logo and scenery dominate the
  first view; specific premise, screenshots, and features follow.
- https://www.rockstargames.com/VI: large intact branded artwork with a clear
  primary action; media and news follow the introduction.
- https://www.nomanssky.com/: game imagery paired with concrete activities.
- https://www.hollowknight.com/: specific descriptions paired with game scenes.

These are design observations and recommendations, not measured conversion
results. The reference sites' artwork, source code, fonts, and identity were
not reused.

## Result

The website now uses a compact charcoal header, a large official game logo,
clear promotional scene, direct Roblox action, and a persistent plain-language
development notice. News appears before the longer game explanation. A compact
status panel states the platform and unannounced release date. The first post
uses a direct welcome title and explains what will be posted.

Removed the pale gradient wash, large generic slogan heading, ornamental
eyebrows, numbered marketing steps, and repeated closing pitch. The visual
language uses the existing Builder fonts, game artwork, blue action colour,
and warm gold from the logo.

The media gallery uses retained early Studio Edit captures, labelled as such.
These are development images, not evidence of gameplay acceptance. The
Hamsturtle image omits the rightmost 200 pixels containing distant placeholder
models. Original captures remain unchanged. See artwork-manifest.json for
source paths, dimensions, transformations, and hashes.

## Updating

Keep dated posts factual and specific. Say what changed and what remains
unfinished. Avoid inventing work to fill a posting schedule. Preserve the
separation between promotional art, Studio previews, and released gameplay.
